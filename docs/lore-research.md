# Lore expansion research notes

Reviewed: 2026-11-04. The expansion adds 87 concise original summaries to the existing 36-entry catalogue, for 123 entries, using 56 source records for the new material. It also adds 18 curated connections to ordinary concepts.

## Scope and method

The research prioritizes HoYoverse character profiles, developer-authored PlayStation Blog articles, publisher press releases on Games Press, and released item descriptions, readables, and mission text. Community wiki pages locate and cross-check setting material and retain their community-reference label; hosting a page on HoYoLAB alone does not make its author official. Some official pages are JavaScript applications, so the research also used indexed descriptions and reproduced game text. Sources are linked on each entry rather than copied into a scraped archive.

The selected release span is 1.0–4.5. Later regions and political changes are included where supported, but a release label identifies the summarized material rather than completeness. Introductory setting and later revelations have separate entries. For example, a simulation analogy may use the public Simulated Universe research premise; it must never automatically retrieve Amphoreus’s hidden nature. In-universe intelligence ratings remain attributed assessments. Faction ideals, citizenship, species, individual personalities, and gameplay Paths are kept distinct. Fictional food or medical descriptions are not real recipes, nutrition, or treatment advice.

The new summaries are short paraphrases, usually covering one identity, institution, custom, location, or event. They avoid canonical dialogue quotations, invented precision, unnecessary biological generalizations, and unsupported claims about every member of a culture. Source pages can change; review the relevant passage before extending a summary.

## Coverage

- Worlds and places: local districts, Xianzhou ship differences, Amphorean city-states, off-route civilizations, and selected 4.x settings.
- Food and culture: regional staples and beverages, street commerce, public leisure, festivals, performance, and food publicity.
- Politics: commissions, Family lineages, corporate divisions, independent organizations, autonomy, and selected disputed policies.
- Lifeforms: biological, inorganic, spiritual, and imagination-based beings, with explicit limits on generalization.
- Characters and history: introductory identities, relationships, ancient disasters, and separated story developments.
